GUIDELINES FOR ONLINE REGISTRATION

Online registration for admissions to classes KG I to Class IX is open in Indian Schools in the capital area for the academic year 2026-2027.

Please go through the following instructions before filling up the on line registration form:

  1. This online registration form is meant for Indian Nationals seeking new admissions in Indian Schools in the capital area for the academic year 2026-2027.
  2. Online registration is mandatory. There is only one application form required for one child; system will not accept any duplication.
  3. A unique login registration number and password will be generated on submission of the application form. This will be sent to the email address and mobile number given in the application form.
  4. A non-refundable processing fee of OMR 15/- will be payable on each application.
  5. Online application form is required even for sibling's admission. To get the sibling preference in the same school, parent should essentially choose the same school as the first option.
  6. Tentative vacancies in different schools posted on the site may be checked by parents to know the number of seats available in different classes.
  7. The choice of Schools is limited to the availability of seats in the class for which admission is sought.
  8. Parents are advised to go through Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) section for more clarification, if required.
  9. Parents seeking inter-school transfers of their wards within Schools in the capital area are required to fill the 'Inter-School Transfer Application form at https://forms.gle/P29avN2BoVufqWGz5
  10. Parents of other nationalities seeking admissions in Indian Schools in the capital area are required to fill the 'Admission to Other Nationalities form at https://forms.gle/hEUAnuLePfyTveD89
Does the child have a valid Indian Passport?
⚠️ Non-Indian passport holders must apply through the Admission to Other Nationalities form.
